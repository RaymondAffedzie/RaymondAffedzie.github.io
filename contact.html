Frequently Asked
Questions

How quickly can I get started with HosCare?

After your demo and subscription, we typically complete setup and training within 1–2 weeks. Smaller properties can often go live within a few days.

Do you offer custom development services?

Yes! We provide custom development and integration services for clients with specific requirements beyond our standard products.

What kind of support do you provide?

All plans include email support. Professional and Enterprise plans include priority support with faster response times and phone support options.

Can I migrate my existing data to your system?

Absolutely! Our team will assist with data migration from your existing systems to ensure a smooth transition with no data loss.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a personalized demo to show you all features. For qualified prospects, we can arrange a pilot program to test the system in your environment.
