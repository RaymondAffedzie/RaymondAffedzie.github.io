Cookie Policy

Information about how we use cookies and similar technologies

Last Updated: April 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit our website. They allow us to recognize your device and remember your preferences. Cookies are widely used to make websites more functional and personalized.

2. Why We Use Cookies

iRBbA Devs uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in to your account
  • Preferences: To remember your theme choice, language, and other settings
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our website
  • Performance: To optimize website speed and functionality
  • Marketing: To deliver targeted advertisements and track campaign effectiveness
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraudulent activity

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as:

  • User authentication and security
  • Account access and session management
  • Form submission and error prevention

Duration: Session (deleted when browser closes) or up to 12 months

3.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

We use these cookies to collect information about how you use our website, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Links clicked and navigation patterns
  • Device type and browser information
  • Geographic location and language preferences

Providers: Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar services

Duration: Up to 24 months

3.3 Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your choices to provide a personalized experience:

  • Theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Language selection
  • Content preferences
  • Accessibility settings

Duration: Up to 1 year

3.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements and track marketing campaigns:

  • Retargeting pixels
  • Conversion tracking
  • Social media integration
  • Lead pixel tracking

Providers: Facebook, LinkedIn, Google Ads, or similar platforms

Duration: Up to 24 months

3.5 Third-Party Cookies

Third parties such as analytics providers and advertisers may set cookies on our site. We do not control these cookies, and they are governed by the third party's privacy policy. Common third parties include:

  • Google Analytics
  • Facebook Pixel
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag
  • Hotjar

4. Similar Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use:

  • Web Beacons: Transparent pixels used to track website activity
  • Local Storage: Browser storage for persistent data without using cookies
  • Session Storage: Temporary storage for the current browser session
  • Cross-Device Tracking: To recognize users across multiple devices

5. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, we collect your consent for non-essential cookies (performance, marketing, preferences). You can:

  • Accept all cookies by clicking "Accept All"
  • Manage preferences by clicking "Manage Preferences"
  • Reject non-essential cookies by clicking "Reject"

Essential cookies are always active for website functionality.

6. How to Control Cookies

6.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and Other Site Data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and Other Site Data

6.2 Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of interest-based advertising:

  • Google Ads Settings: https://www.google.com/settings/ads
  • Facebook Ads Preferences: https://www.facebook.com/ads/preferences
  • Network Advertising Initiative: https://optout.networkadvertising.org

6.3 Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. While we recognize this signal, we may not be able to honor all such requests as some services require cookies to function properly.

7. Cookie Retention Period

Cookie retention varies by type:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain for a set period (typically 1-24 months) or until manually deleted

8. International Data Transfers

Information collected via cookies may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws. By using our website, you consent to such transfers.

9. GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Union, we comply with GDPR requirements for cookie consent:

  • We obtain explicit consent before placing non-essential cookies
  • We provide clear information about cookie purposes and duration
  • You can withdraw consent at any time
  • Essential cookies do not require consent

10. CCPA Compliance

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • You have the right to know what cookies we use
  • You can opt-out of the "sale" of personal information via cookies
  • You can delete cookies and browsing history

11. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, regulations, or our practices. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page or sending you an email notification.
