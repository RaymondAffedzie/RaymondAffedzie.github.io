Our Journey

From Idea to Impact

2022

iRBbA Devs Founded

Born from a simple observation: institutions across Africa were struggling with outdated, inefficient systems. A team of engineers and industry professionals came together to bridge this gap — and build something better.

2023

HosCare Launched

Through close collaboration with hotel operators, we launched HosCare — a full hospitality management system addressing real pain points: streamlined check-ins, integrated POS, automated housekeeping, and powerful analytics dashboards.

2024

500+ Users Milestone

HosCare validated our approach: deep industry research, user-centered design, and a commitment to scalable, reliable systems. We reached 500+ active users across multiple properties.

2026

Multi-Product Expansion

Expanding our proven methodology to education, banking, and enterprise ERP. Our product suite grows while our founding principles stay unchanged: quality, user experience, and lasting partnerships.
