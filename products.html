HosCare

An all-in-one hospitality management platform — from front desk to housekeeping, POS to financial reporting, unified in a single cloud-native system.

Cloud-Native Mobile-Ready PCI Compliant Multi-Currency

Front Desk Management

Streamlined check-in/check-out, reservation management, guest profiles, and real-time room assignment.

Integrated POS System

Point-of-sale for restaurant, bar, spa, and hotel services with inventory tracking and billing integration.

Housekeeping Module

Assign tasks, track room status, manage cleaning schedules, and coordinate maintenance with mobile staff access.

Reservation System
